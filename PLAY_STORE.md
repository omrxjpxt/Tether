## 3. Full Description (max 4000 chars)

```
Build habits that stick.

Tether is a calm, personal habit, consistency, and focus application designed around the proven principle of habit stacking:

"After I [current routine], I will [new micro-action]."

Instead of abstract schedules or overwhelming checklists, Tether anchors your new intentions to existing physical and situational cues. 

Tether is built with zero noise: no social feeds, no artificial intelligence, no cloud databases, no advertisements, and no gamified pressure. Just clean, calm tools to help you show up every day.

====================
KEY FEATURES
====================

🔗 HABIT STACKING
Anchor new habits to current habits using our intuitive stacking flow. Define your trigger and micro-action clearly so following through becomes second nature.

📅 TODAY DASHBOARD
Focus on what matters today. View your active stacks, track your 7-day completion history, and check off completions with tactile haptic feedback.

🔄 NEVER MISS TWICE
Missing once is an accident; missing twice is the start of a new habit. Tether emphasizes gentle recovery and restarting without streak shaming or punishment.

⏱️ DEEP FOCUS TIMER
A clean, distraction-free countdown timer for deep work intervals (25m, 50m, 90m presets or custom lengths). Resilient against app backgrounding and device lock.

📊 WEEKLY REVIEW & REFLECTIONS
Review your week from Monday to Sunday. View completion rates, aggregated focus minutes, and journal your weekly observations in a persistent reflection scratchpad.

🔔 LOCAL REMINDERS
Set gentle reminder times for your habits. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device—never sent from remote servers.

🎨 HIGH-CONTRAST LIGHT & DARK THEMES
A thoughtfully crafted visual design featuring warm ivory light surfaces, deep obsidian dark surfaces, and clear typography hierarchy.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
Tether works completely without internet access. Your habits, focus sessions, and reflections live exclusively on your device. We do not collect, track, or sell your personal data.

💾 COMPLETE DATA PORTABILITY
Export and restore your complete data history anytime as a standard JSON backup file via your device's native share sheet.

====================
OUR PHILOSOPHY
====================

- No accounts or logins required.
- No cloud synchronization or third-party servers.
- No ads, subscriptions, or paywalls.
- No push notification spam or artificial streaks.

Build consistent routines with intention. Download Tether today.
```

## 5. Permissions Explanation for Reviewers

- **Post Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to schedule local habit reminders configured by the user.
- **Schedule Exact Alarm (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Used to trigger reminders accurately at the designated time.
- **Receive Boot Completed (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Restores user-configured reminder schedules when the device restarts.
- **Vibrate (`VIBRATE`):** Delivers tactile haptic feedback during timer state transitions and habit completion.
